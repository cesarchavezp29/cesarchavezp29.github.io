##### Abstract

This paper develops a two-frontier task model to analyze how robots and AI affect labor markets and inequality. Challenges the conventional view that automation can be summarized by a single statistic, showing that different types of automation (robots vs. AI) have distinct effects on wages, employment, and inequality.
