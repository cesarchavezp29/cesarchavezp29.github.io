##### Abstract

I analyze all Nobel Prizes in Economic Sciences awarded between 1969 and 2025 to document patterns in geographic concentration, institutional affiliations, methodological approaches, and research fields. The analysis reveals increasing recognition of empirical and experimental methods over purely theoretical work.
