##### Abstract

This IMF Working Paper examines ethnic income gaps in Peru during the country's sustained economic growth period from 2000-2020. Co-authored with Gonzalo Salinas and Zahid Yuri, the paper analyzes labor market outcomes and inequality dynamics across ethnic groups.
