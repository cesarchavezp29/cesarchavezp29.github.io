##### Abstract

This paper challenges the conventional wisdom that export-led growth is the primary mechanism through which trade reduces poverty in developing countries. Using panel data from 18 Latin American countries, I show that import liberalization has larger poverty-reducing effects than export expansion through the consumption channel.
