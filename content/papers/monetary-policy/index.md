##### Abstract

This paper elaborates a new measure of a monetary policy shock, with profitability of firms measured as the ratio between Net Income and Total Assets and Net Profits. Using Local Projections methodology, I examine the heterogeneous effects of monetary policy across industries in Peru using publicly traded firm data.
